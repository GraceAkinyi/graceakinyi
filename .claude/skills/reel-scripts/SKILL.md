# Reel Scripts

Grace's saved script formulas for short vertical videos. When she asks for a script or post idea:
1. Pick the formula that fits (or the one she names), and fill it in for her topic, in her voice: calm, warm and expert, with no hype.
2. Give her the finished script beat by beat: **what to show** and **what to say**, short enough to read off a phone, with the total length (aim for 20 to 45 s).
3. Add the on-screen text for each beat in her brand style (see `/video-editor`: gold + cream, Playfair Display italic for key words, DM Sans for the rest).
4. Never put a fact or number on screen that she hasn't confirmed.

When she then sends the footage, edit it with `/video-editor`, using the beats below as the cut points and caption plan.

---

## Formula 1: "Moodboard list" (relatable caption post)

| Beat | What to do | Script |
|---|---|---|
| **Intro** | Start with a bold mood claim. | "If your brain feels like a moodboard, wait for this post idea." |
| **Setup** | Show a "list" caption format on screen. | "We're turning tiny daily moments into a relatable caption in one shot." |
| **Core** | Explain the formula in steps. | "Pick 6 moments, 2 themes, then end with one line that feels personal." |
| **Show** | Film yourself reading the list. | "Show the list, point to each item, then pause on the last 'that's me' line." |
| **Peak** | Add a surprising twist line. | "Now swap one item with an unexpected detail — watch reactions spike." |
| **Outro** | Ask viewers to comment their item. | "Comment your 1 moment — then I'll make part two!" |

**Editing notes for this formula:**
- **Intro:** the hook text is on screen from frame 1, with "moodboard" in gold Playfair italic.
- **Setup and Show:** build the list on screen one item at a time, timed to when she points at each one. Use cream cards or numbered lines with a small gold number.
- **Show (last line):** hold the "that's me" line about 0.8 s longer and punch in to 108%.
- **Peak:** make the twist item visually different (gold highlight or a strikethrough-and-replace) so the surprise reads without sound.
- **Outro:** end card "Comment your 1 moment" with "part two" in gold. This is a comment-keyword CTA, which the viral playbook in `/video-editor` favours.
